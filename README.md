Neon Coast Cover Studio — turn your photos into multi-panel poster cover art with a sunset or neon colour grade, and export it as a PNG to share. It runs offline in a desktop browser from its own static files: your photos never leave your machine.

- **Any photo:** upload your own (drag-and-drop or file picker), or try the four built-in samples. Crop and position each panel by dragging, scrolling to zoom, or with the sliders; fill several panels with one photo or several.
- **The poster look:** three multi-panel layouts (Hero + 3, Hero + 2, Grid 2 × 2), bold poster lettering in three styles (Block, Outline, Neon glow) with four title colours, and sunset / neon / golden colour grades with a strength slider.
- **Fast:** one-click looks (Sunset Heist, Neon Nights, Golden Hour), then one click exports a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080 — or all three at once.
- **Original:** every backdrop and sample photo was made for this entry with an image model, and the lettering is drawn from system fonts. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no dependencies, no network. `main.ts` (Node 22) hands the app in: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
